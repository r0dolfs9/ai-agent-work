### 3. O02 — Independent evaluation & outcome-audit for support agents (score 69.0)

- **Pricing hypothesis:** €3–8k per audit; €1–3k/month continuous sampling (HYPOTHESIS)
- **Setup cost:** Low–medium: rubric, sampling tooling, data-processing agreement templates
- **Operating burden:** Medium: human graders, data protection obligations
- **Margin:** 40–60% (human grading labour) (ASSUMPTION)
- **Dependencies:** Customer willing to share transcripts (GDPR DPA)
- **Competitors & substitutes:** Braintrust, Arize, LangSmith (tools, not independent audit); vendor QA features; BPO QA teams
- **Defensibility:** Medium: independence + calibrated benchmarks across vendors
- **Platform risk:** Low–medium

- **Score rationale:** evidence: mechanisms documented (S037,S071) but no buyer demand observed; pain: resolution dominates cost (CALC L36); wtp: plausible via invoice savings; competition: tools exist but not independent audits
