## The one-paragraph answer

There is a credible opportunity to **explore**, meaning a capped test rather than starting a business. It lies in **services sold to people and companies who run AI agents**, not in products that agents buy themselves.
- **If you code professionally:** explore **coding-agent enablement with a measured pilot** first.
- **If you don't:** start with **small AI workflow automations** through a freelance marketplace.
- **If you have a security background:** a **limited agent risk review** is a strong alternative. It is not an audit or certification.

Testing takes about **50–60 hours and ≤€150 over 6–10 weeks**. The biggest unknown is whether buyers will choose *you*.
- **As a newcomer without warm contacts:** the first year pays only about €3–11 an hour.
- **With a network:** it pays €23–36 an hour.

So your access to buyers matters more than the route, and your own employment or contracting rate is the benchmark to beat. **The one decision needed from you is at the end of the decision brief (§10).**

## What changed since v1.1 (in one minute)

- **Latvian VAT corrected.**
  - Buying foreign AI tools does **not** force VAT registration since July 2025: a special registration exists [S122].
  - Selling to an EU VAT-registered business does force it.
  - The micro-enterprise tax stays available for non-EU clients [S124].
- **Shortlist rebuilt.** Coding-agent enablement now leads (for coders). Security review is conditional on credentials. Support-agent evaluation is deferred. A simpler adjacent route (workflow automations) has been added.
- **New economics.** Every route is now modelled through to the owner's take-home pay per hour, for a newcomer and for someone with a network, including a harsh adverse case. The base was revised downward after independent review.
- **Fresh checks.** Atlas shutdown, Anthropic revenue and bans, x402 volumes and Gartner figures were re-checked at source. The Gartner alternative-host access path was discontinued.
- **Independent reviewers.** Two independent AI reviewers checked the package, one factual and one commercial. Their findings changed the economics, the outreach rules and the commitment thresholds (review document).
- **Previous versions preserved.** `archive/v1.1/` (and local git tag `v1.1`), and `archive/v1.0/`.
