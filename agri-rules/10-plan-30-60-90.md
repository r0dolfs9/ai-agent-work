## K.3 Measurable continue / revise / stop conditions (at S8)

| Decision | Conditions (all measured in S0–S7) |
|---|---|
| **Continue** to the full build | Extraction pipeline PASS, or CONDITIONAL with affordable 100 % review; **and** challenger catch rate ≥ 70 % with shared errors < 30 %; **and** median review time ≤ 0.6 h/cell; **and** lawyer review found no systematic misreading left unfixed; **and** the measured full-build cost fits the owner's cap |
| **Revise** | Any one of: review time 0.6–1.2 h/cell (reduce scope: drop ORG/VET duplicates, fewer layers); gold disagreement 15–25 % (reword questions); a layer with access or terms problems (swap Italy ↔ Denmark per 03 §D.3) |
| **Stop** | Any one of: no extraction candidate reaches CONDITIONAL; review time > 1.2 h/cell (≈1,200 h for the full build); lawyers find that the comparison unit itself misleads (e.g. most cells are collective-agreement dependent); or a load-bearing source is not lawfully usable |

## K.4 The smallest credible first action

**Run S0 on 3 questions × 10 layers by hand.**
- Cost: €0, ~40 hours.
- What it tests: the riskiest assumption in the whole plan. That assumption is not "can AI do this?" but "**can a human produce a defensible gold answer per cell, and how long does it take?**". Every cost, timeline and accuracy claim downstream depends on that number.
- What it delivers: the first real gold data for the benchmark, plus permission findings for the three sources whose terms are unknown (EE, LV, PL).
